# Code of Conduct

Pdf++ contributors and participants must keep project spaces respectful, technical, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Give actionable feedback with evidence and reproducible examples.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Disclose conflicts of interest where relevant.
- Protect private security reports and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or repeated personal attacks are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Serious or repeated incidents should be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
